# Development roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — foundation (this repository):** separated systems, fake timing
   events, deterministic Normal generation, basic four-lane editor, audio asset
   reference/preview, and intermediate/V-Slice preview JSON.
2. **Phase 2 — real timing:** user-selected audio, decoder boundary, BPM/time
   signature model, onset detection, quantization, and analysis fixture tests.
3. **Phase 3 — musical sources:** external/native stem separation, vocals/bass/
   drums/melody/other classifiers, confidence data, and stem-selection UI.
4. **Phase 4 — chart intelligence:** playability scoring, pattern vocabulary,
   purposeful Easy/Normal/Hard policies, Solo/VS, and configurable source owner/
   ignored mappings.
5. **Phase 5 — full chart editor:** waveform/timeline virtualization, scrolling,
   sustain handles, drag/multi-select, command-based undo/redo, autosave, and
   section regeneration that preserves manual edits.
6. **Phase 6 — stage/set editor:** image backgrounds, normal stage props and
   character assets, schema validation, preview/playtesting, and packaging.
7. **Phase 7 — platform polish:** pointer/gesture completion, adaptive layouts,
   desktop/Android/iOS testing, memory/performance budgets, export/distribution,
   and a documented external-analysis workflow for mobile.
